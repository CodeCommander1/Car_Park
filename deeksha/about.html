About Deeksha Group of Education

Established in 2011, Deeksha Group of Education has been a beacon of hope for thousands of aspirants preparing for government competitive examinations and academic excellence.

Our Mission

To empower every aspirant with the knowledge, skills, and confidence needed to excel in government competitive examinations and academic subjects, ensuring quality education remains accessible to all.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and preferred educational institute for competitive exam preparation and academic coaching, known for our innovative teaching methods and exceptional success rates.

Manoj Kumar

Director & Founder

Message from Director

"When I founded Deeksha Group of Education in 2011, my vision was simple yet profound: to create an institution that would bridge the gap between aspirants and their dreams of serving the nation through government jobs and academic excellence.

Over the years, we have witnessed thousands of students transform their lives through dedicated preparation and our expert guidance. Our success lies not just in the numbers but in the confidence we instill in every student who walks through our doors.

We believe that quality education should be accessible to all, regardless of economic background. This philosophy drives our commitment to maintaining affordable fee structures while never compromising on the quality of education we provide.

Today, as we continue to evolve and adapt to changing educational needs, our core values remain unchanged: dedication, excellence, and accessibility. Join us in this journey of transformation and success."

Our Journey

Present

Continuing to serve thousands of aspirants with over 5000 successful candidates to date.
