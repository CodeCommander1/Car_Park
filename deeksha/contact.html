Frequently Asked Questions

What are the admission requirements?

For competitive coaching, graduation is preferred but not mandatory. For academic coaching (8-10), students should be currently enrolled in respective classes.

Do you provide study materials?

Yes, we provide comprehensive study materials, practice tests, and previous year question papers for all our courses.

What is the batch size?

We maintain small batch sizes of 20-30 students to ensure personalized attention and effective learning.

Are there any payment installments?

For competitive coaching, the fee is ₹7,500 one-time. For academic coaching, it's ₹600 per month per subject.

Do you provide job placement assistance?

We provide comprehensive guidance on exam patterns, application processes, and interview preparation to help you succeed.

Can I visit the institute before admission?

Absolutely! We encourage prospective students to visit our campus, meet faculty, and see our facilities before making a decision.

Need Immediate Assistance?

Our counselors are available to help you choose the right course and answer all your questions.
